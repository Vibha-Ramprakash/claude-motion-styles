The 7 style prompts

Paste one at the end of the build prompt. Swap the bits in [brackets] for your own.

STYLE 1 OF 7

Glass Morph

Use it for: product launches + feature demos

THE STYLE PROMPT

Make an 8-second product film for [your product]. One dark shape morphs from a dot into a call pill, an agent card, a glass work-order card and a phone, then back into the logo. Liquid-glass UI on soft satin, a cursor clicks every step, cuts on a 120 BPM beat.

PRO TIP
Give it your logo SVG + brand hex codes so the morph lands on your real logo.

STYLE 2 OF 7

Pixel Game

Use it for: playful teasers + milestones

THE STYLE PROMPT

Make an 8-second pixel-art side-scroller. A tiny runner sprints through three levels (city, desert, snowy aurora), jumping [obstacles from your world] and grabbing a coin on every beat. Dithered skies, level-up banners, a chiptune synced to every jump, ending on a loading bar.

PRO TIP
Ask for 3 levels in 3 palettes and a hurdle per level, so it never repeats.
#3A1A2C#D97757#F6C945#2E8A99
STYLE 3 OF 7

Hand-drawn Recipe

Use it for: step-by-step processes + how-tos

THE STYLE PROMPT

Make an 8-second recipe animation for [your recipe or process]. Wobbly hand-drawn outlines that boil, a chunky outlined title, a glass with oz markers. Bottles pour on curved streams, a card counts each step, dots tick off with checkmarks. Ice clinks, fizz pops, end on a cheers card.

PRO TIP
Paste your real steps + amounts. It builds the step cards and counters for you.
#F26B2A#3D2B2F#D9EDF3#FBF8F3
STYLE 4 OF 7

Paper Collage

Use it for: storytelling + big announcements

THE STYLE PROMPT

Make an 8-second Vox-style paper collage about [your story]. Halftone cut-outs with white sticker borders, torn kraft and newspaper strips, masking tape, a sketch circled in red, a 3-2-1 countdown with a new texture every beat and a ransom-note headline. Paper and typewriter sounds on every cut.

PRO TIP
Use public-domain archive photos and ask it to cut them out + add halftone.

STYLE 5 OF 7

One-Line Ink

Use it for: explainers + brand intros

THE STYLE PROMPT

Make an 8-second one-line drawing on graph paper. A single unbroken ink line draws [3 to 4 objects from your world], flowing one into the next, then writes [your sign-off] in cursive. The camera follows the pen, a watercolour wash blooms behind each drawing, a marimba note on each.

PRO TIP
List your objects in order. It plans one path that links them all.

STYLE 6 OF 7

Hologram HUD

Use it for: product specs + hardware reveals

THE STYLE PROMPT

Make an 8-second hologram spec reveal. Scan [your product] bottom-up into a glowing cyan wireframe on a holo platform. Target boxes lock onto three parts, dashed lines lead to spec panels whose numbers roll up in orange, then a thin title and price glitch in.

PRO TIP
List 3 real specs with units. It builds the target locks + number rolls.

STYLE 7 OF 7

ASCII Terminal

Use it for: dev + tech launches

THE STYLE PROMPT

Make an 8-second ASCII animation inside a terminal. A 3D [object] made of characters drops in, bounces twice with a squash, then rolls across an ASCII landscape of mountains, stars and km markers while the camera follows. Its pattern spins as it rolls; a tiny progress bar ticks to 100%.

PRO TIP
Give the object a tiny story (drop, bounce, roll) instead of a plain spin.

Notes I gave between drafts

Every one of these took a few rounds. These notes got them there faster.

Match the reference's detailIf it looks flatter than the reference, name what's missing: grain, layers, shadows, camera.
Give every video its own endingDon't let every clip land on the same tagline. Vary the last beat and the font.
Readable on a phoneBig type, short lines, nothing tiny in the corners.
Frame the whole objectAsk the camera to frame each drawing or product, not chase one point.
A story, not a loopDrop, bounce, roll beats spin. Give the motion a beginning and an end.
Sound on every movePops, clicks, thuds and chimes on cue. I skip whooshes.
